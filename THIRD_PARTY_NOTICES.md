## FriBiDi 1.0.16

mojit distributes an unmodified, separately replaceable Windows x64 build of
[GNU FriBiDi](https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi), licensed under
LGPL-2.1-or-later.

The complete corresponding source archive is distributed in
`vendor/fribidi/source/fribidi-1.0.16.tar.xz`. The license is included at
`src/mojit/_native/licenses/COPYING-LGPL-2.1-or-later.txt`; exact source and
binary hashes and the build recipe are recorded in `src/mojit/_native/manifest.toml`.

NumPy and Pillow are installed as separate Python distributions. Their own package
metadata contains their applicable licenses and notices.
